### 2. Approach Described in the Retail Supply Chain Case Study

- **Source:** Retrieval Agent — `retail-supply-chain-case-study.pdf` (Sources 2 & 4)
- **Label:** 📄 Retrieval Agent

The case study describes the following approach:

1. **Centralized Demand Forecasting System** — Ingests point-of-sale data nightly and generates automated purchase order recommendations, factoring in day-of-week seasonality, local weather forecasts, and historical promotional lift.

2. **Weekly Variance Review Process** — Allows store managers to flag local anomalies to ensure accuracy and responsiveness.

3. **Phased Rollout** — Deployed across three regions over four months, beginning with the five highest-volume stores to validate the model before company-wide expansion.

4. **Training & Support** — Included two half-day training sessions for store managers and a dedicated support channel for the first six weeks post-launch in each region.

5. **Manager Override Authority** — Purchase orders were *not* fully automated; managers retained override ability, which significantly improved adoption over a previously attempted fully automated pilot.
